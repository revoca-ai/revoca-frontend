# Revoca

### // The ultimate context layer for the enterprise

---

> **The Vision:** We are moving businesses closer to the "One-Person Company" — by eliminating knowledge silos forever. Revoca is the ultimate employee who has been there since Day 1, observing and learning everything.

---

## The Problem

Enterprise teams are hemorrhaging productivity to a silent killer: **institutional amnesia.**

- **People go offline. Teams stall.** Critical decisions and task status are locked behind one person. The rest waits.
- **Knowledge lives nowhere.** Scattered across Slack threads, Jira tickets, GitHub PRs — disconnected, unstructured, and effectively gone.
- **Onboarding never ends.** Every new hire, same slow process. Tribal knowledge evaporates every time someone leaves.

> 💡 The information exists. The problem is that no one can access it — except the one person who isn't there.

---

## How Revoca Fixes This

### ◈ 24×7 Uninterrupted Workflows

Your devs go offline. Revoca keeps them present. Query any past decision, task status, or reasoning — instantly — even when the person is away. **No one is ever blocked.**

### ◎ Read Between the Lines

Revoca doesn't just search — it **reasons**. It traces what decisions were made, why they were made, and what context drove them, across your entire knowledge base.

### ◐ Isolated & Secure by Design

One unified knowledge base. Multiple agents with **strictly isolated access**. A customer support agent cannot access internal proprietary data. Zero data leakage is our absolute priority.
